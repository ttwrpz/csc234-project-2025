\section{What we shipped (v1.0)}

Sprint 4 implemented the ecosystem redesign that resolved the professor's
post-Sprint-3 feedback (the user-driven pivot from ``wilting plants for
negative moods'' to ``plants never die, every mood is weather''). Twelve
in-scope work breakdown items landed:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{3.6 Mood Score} --- pure-Dart $S_t = v \cdot i/5$, range $[-1, +1]$.
\item \textbf{4.2 Garden Health EWMA} --- $H_t = 0.15 S_t + 0.85 H_{t-1}$, weekly reset to $H_0 = 0$.
\item \textbf{4.3 Daily Atmosphere} --- sunny / calm / light-rain / storm driven by $\overline{S}_{\text{today}}$; resets midnight.
\item \textbf{4.4 Day/Night theme} --- follow-device-theme + follow-device-time strategies.
\item \textbf{5.3 Pattern Engine} --- five pure-Dart algorithms: Mann--Kendall, sliding 5-of-7, three-consecutive, z-score, CUSUM. Triggers fire internally; surface is Sprint 5.
\item \textbf{6.1 Weekly Harvest} --- seven-day archive cycle; copy ``harvest / complete / new chapter'' (never ``delete / reset / lost'').
\item \textbf{6.2 Token economy} --- mood-agnostic earning, 5--10 tokens/day cap, no streak punishment, cosmetic-only spending.
\item \textbf{7.2 Dark mode} polish; \textbf{8.2} widget + 24 golden tests under 4\% pixel-tolerance.
\end{itemize}

ADR-0010 (``Ecosystem model --- plants never die'') and ADR-0011 (``Client-side
Pattern Engine'') accompany the redesign \autocite{neff2003selfcompassion,linehan1993dbt,hayes1999act,white1990narrative,smit2022ewma}.

\section{Demo flow on May 12}

The demo walked through (1) logging a Joy entry at intensity 4 to observe
the Mood Score arithmetic; (2) logging a Sad entry to verify the
bounded-daily-shift property $|\Delta H| \leq 0.15$; (3) the Patterns chart
re-rendering; (4) cycling all five plant tiers via Settings $\to$ Debug; (5)
verifying WCAG 2.2 AA contrast in dark mode; (6) force-harvesting the
active week to verify the archive copy.

\section{What went well}

Architect-front-loaded ADRs prevented mid-sprint design ambiguity. The
formula-precise spec (every threshold and tolerance written down)
eliminated bikeshedding. Golden-test discipline scaled to 24 deterministic
tests across five plant tiers and four atmospheres.

\section{What was hard}

The Mann--Kendall quantization deviation surfaced only after the test ran
with synthetic data --- the spec author had not realised that integer-valued
$S$ quantizes $Z$. The Bangkok Firestore region rejection (\texttt{asia-southeast3} not supported as a v1 or v2 Firestore trigger) cost two hours on Day 6 of polish; resolved by converting \texttt{sendCheerUpPush} to \texttt{onCall}. Two parallel agents collided on a shared working tree on Day 2; recovery cost 30 minutes and triggered the now-standard \texttt{isolation:"worktree"} dispatch discipline.

\section{What the human team caught that the agents missed}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Ghost flowers after wipe} --- the old \texttt{PlantTierGroup} painted three buds unconditionally; the user-test surfaced it. Replaced with \texttt{GardenBed}.
\item \textbf{Rain only fills half the canvas} --- atmosphere overlay was composited to the bed slot instead of the SkyHeader's \texttt{Positioned.fill}.
\item \textbf{Stray sun-ray lines} --- prototype debris committed by accident; the sky gradient already carries the brightness signal.
\item \textbf{AI text analyze fired on 2--3 char drafts} --- no input-debounce in the controller; user testing surfaced wasted Gemini calls. Added \texttt{aiSuggestionMinCharsProvider} (default 12).
\end{itemize}

\section{Going into Sprint 5}

The pivot is shipped; the safety net is not yet wired. Sprint 5 surfaces
the Pattern Engine triggers as Tiered Intervention notifications,
builds the Quote Library with Safety Filter and curated Tier 3 pool, and
ships the Bipolar/medical disclaimer service \autocite{nahum2018jitai,cheng2019gamification}.
